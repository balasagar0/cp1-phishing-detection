# Chapter 5. Limitations

> Generated by `scripts/render_docs.py` from `reports/final_numbers.json` (build `cp1-final`, commit `18ba1d9`). Edit `docs/templates/report/05_limitations.md`, not this file. Every number comes from the reports; none is typed.

Each limitation below is measured on build `cp1-final`.

1. **Correlation is measured only on simulated bundles.** The events are real; the campaigns are constructed. On them the deployed engine
   has F1 92.0% and a false-positive rate of 11.1%, against 91.0% and 14.1% for
   single-channel alerting. No real coordinated bundle has been collected yet (R2-10).
2. **The risk is not a calibrated probability.** The alert line 0.5 is tied to the simulation's mix (49.6% of the test bundles are malicious).
3. **The correlation model is not stable on borderline bundles.** A retrain moved TC-06 from 0.595 to
   0.456 while the simulated F1 moved from 92.0% to 92.1%.
4. **Email does not transfer across sources.** F1 82.2% on unseen corpora; false-positive rates there of
   1.8% to 28.0%; 23.5% false alarms on 4,069 real legitimate emails of one user. The labels mix
   spam and fraud with phishing.
5. **The URL fix costs recall.** Bad URLs hosted on popular domains are caught less often: 49.9% before the fix,
   30.7% after. Login pages on small unknown sites are still likely to be flagged.
6. **SMS smishing is a small class.** 105 test messages; F1 interval 82.3%-91.7%.
7. **The correlated score can be lower than the best single score** (D-18): a lone high score next to weaker events is pulled down a little.
8. **A detector false positive stays a false positive.** Correlation does not repair it (TC-08).
9. **Voice and video are not assessed.** No detector was trained.
10. **The corpora are mostly English and several years old.** Regional-language and current phishing are under-represented.
11. **Speed was measured on the build laptop**, one request at a time; the demo laptop and concurrent load are untested.
